Workwear for the salons, barbers and spas of the Lincolnshire coast

A resort town on the Lincolnshire coast, Skegness runs its salon trade through the streets back from the seafront. Lumley Road, the main shopping street, holds the densest run of barbers, hair salons and beauty rooms between the clock tower and the station. Roman Bank carries further hairdressers and a hair and beauty spa out along the main road, while Algitha Road and High Street add unisex salons and nail bars, and the parades round it off with barbershops serving the town and the holiday trade.

And the trade is overwhelmingly small and independent. The Lumley Road barbers and hair salons, the Roman Bank hairdressers and beauty spa, the Algitha Road salons, the High Street nail bars, and the mobile beauticians and aesthetics practitioners working out across Ingoldmells, Chapel St Leonards and the coastal villages are nearly all sole traders or small teams rather than chains. Almost every one of them puts staff in front of clients in something branded, and how they look is part of the service.

That shapes the job for a uniform supplier. A one-chair barber, a self-employed mobile beautician, a two-room nail studio or a small salon team does not have a buying department or a trade account. They want to choose a few branded pieces, a tunic, a couple of polos, an apron and some clogs, and order them quickly, without setting anything up.

The uniform itself works in two quiet ways. First, presentation: a salon, barber or spa sells a professional, cared-for experience, so a clean, branded tunic or polo tells a Skegness client they are in capable, hygienic hands as soon as they walk in. Second, practical: aprons take the colour, water and product, tunics and polos wipe clean and wash well, and clogs keep a stylist or therapist comfortable through long days on their feet.

And because it is a small, repeat-purchase trade, the range is deliberately narrow and easy to reorder. Tunics, polos, aprons and clogs, branded in-house with the salon name, are the whole kit, and we hold your logo on file so the next order, the next chair or the new junior matches what you already have without anyone in Skegness starting again. It comes down to tunics, polos, aprons and clogs, branded with the salon name across the town's salons, barber chairs and treatment rooms.
