Kitting the salons, barbers and spas of north Hampshire

A garrison town in north-east Hampshire, Aldershot carries its salon trade across the town-centre streets and a ring of suburban parades. Union Street holds a dense run of hair and beauty salons, while Victoria Road and the High Street add their own barbers, hairdressers and nail bars. The Wellington Centre pulls in further hair units and treatment rooms, and out from the middle the North Town and Ash Road parades carry the barbershops and salons serving the estates and the wider garrison district.

And the trade is overwhelmingly small and independent. The Union Street hair and beauty salons, the Victoria Road barbers, the Wellington Centre nail bars, the North Town and Ash hairdressers, and the mobile beauticians and aesthetics practitioners working across the town are nearly all sole traders or small teams rather than chains. Each one puts staff in front of clients, so a sharp, branded look is treated as part of the job.

This shapes what the supplier has to get right. A one-chair barber, a mobile beautician, a two-room nail studio or a small salon team does not have a procurement department or a buying account, so they want to pick a few branded pieces, a tunic, a couple of polos, an apron and some clogs, and order them quickly, with nothing to set up.

The uniform itself does two quiet jobs. The first is presentation: a salon, barber or spa sells a professional, cared-for experience, and a clean, branded tunic or polo tells a Aldershot client they are in capable, hygienic hands the moment they walk in. The second is practical: aprons take the colour, water and product, tunics and polos wipe clean and wash well, and clogs keep a stylist or therapist comfortable through long days on their feet.

And because this is a small, repeat-purchase trade, the range is deliberately narrow and easy to reorder. Tunics, polos, aprons and clogs, branded in-house with the salon name, are the whole kit, and your logo is held on file so the next order, the next chair or a new junior matches what you already have without a Aldershot salon starting again.
