Kitting the salons, barbers and spas of Greater Manchester

A former cotton town south of Bolton, Farnworth packs its hair and beauty trade into the centre. Market Street is the spine, running a dense row of barbers and hair salons past the precinct, with the likes of Torres Barber, New Look Barber and Alan Paul Hairdressers sitting close together. Off the high street, Plodder Lane carries Coconut Hair, Beauty and Body Contour and a string of nail and beauty rooms, while the Highfield, Moses Gate and New Bury estates add their own neighbourhood chairs serving steady local custom.

And the trade is overwhelmingly small and independent. The Market Street barbers and hair salons, the Plodder Lane beauty rooms and nail bars, the Highfield hairdressers and the home studios and mobile beauticians covering Farnworth, Kearsley and Little Hulton are nearly all sole traders or small teams rather than chains. Each one puts staff in front of clients, and looking the part in something branded is simply part of the job.

That shapes the job for a uniform supplier. A one-chair barber, a self-employed mobile beautician, a two-room nail studio or a small salon team does not have a buying department or a trade account. They want to choose a few branded pieces, a tunic, a couple of polos, an apron and some clogs, and order them quickly, without setting anything up.

The uniform itself works in two quiet ways. First, presentation: a salon, barber or spa sells a professional, cared-for experience, so a clean, branded tunic or polo tells a Farnworth client they are in capable, hygienic hands as soon as they walk in. Second, practical: aprons take the colour, water and product, tunics and polos wipe clean and wash well, and clogs keep a stylist or therapist comfortable through long days on their feet.

And because it is a small, repeat-purchase trade, the range stays deliberately narrow and easy to reorder. Tunics, polos, aprons and clogs, branded in-house with the salon name, are the whole kit, and your logo is held on file so the next order, the next chair or a new junior matches what you have without anyone in Farnworth starting again.
