Kitting the salons, barbers and spas of east Durham

A new town on the east Durham coalfield between Seaham and Hartlepool, Peterlee runs its salon trade through the pedestrianised town centre. Yoden Way holds the densest run of hairdressers, barbers and beauty rooms, from walk-in shops to full-service salons along the precinct. Upper Yoden Way adds further hair and beauty studios, while the Castle Dene shopping centre pulls barbers and hair and beauty units in under one roof, and Burnhope Way carries tanning and beauty shops on the edge of the centre.

And the trade is overwhelmingly small and independent. The Yoden Way hairdressers and barbers, the Upper Yoden Way studios, the Castle Dene hair and beauty units, the Burnhope Way tanning rooms, and the mobile beauticians and aesthetics practitioners working out to Horden, Easington and the coast villages are nearly all sole traders or small teams rather than chains. Almost every one of them puts staff in front of clients in something branded, and how they look is part of the service.

That shapes what a uniform supplier needs to do. A one-chair barber, a self-employed mobile beautician, a two-room nail studio or a small salon team does not have a procurement department or a buying account. They want to choose a few branded pieces, a tunic, a couple of polos, an apron and some clogs, and order them quickly and simply, without setting anything up.

The uniform quietly does two jobs. The first is presentation: a salon, barber or spa sells a professional, cared-for experience, and a clean, branded tunic or polo signals to a Peterlee client that they are in capable, hygienic hands. The second is practical: aprons take colour, water and product, tunics and polos wipe clean and wash well, and clogs keep a stylist or therapist comfortable through long days on their feet.

And because it is a small, repeat-purchase trade, the range stays deliberately narrow and easy to reorder. Tunics, polos, aprons and clogs, branded in-house with the salon name, are the whole kit, and your logo is held on file so the next order, the next chair or a new junior matches what you have without anyone in Peterlee starting again.
