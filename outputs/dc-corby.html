Kitting the people who deliver Corby and the Golden Triangle, door to door

A former steel town turned logistics hub, Corby sits in the centre of the Golden Triangle on the A43 and A6003, with the dualled A43 link road feeding the A14 and on to the M1 and M6. Its distribution muscle lies in the Earlstrees and Weldon industrial estates, the vast Magna Park Corby and the Midlands Logistics Park, which runs its own rail freight terminal on the line that once carried the old Eurohub steel trains to St Pancras.

Corby pulls a heavy carrier presence. National parcel and pallet networks run big sheds across Earlstrees, Willowbrook and the logistics parks, while a busy layer of independent same-day and multidrop firms and owner-drivers covers the last mile into Corby, Kettering, Wellingborough and Market Harborough. The town's warehousing and e-commerce fulfilment base keeps the volume high year-round. Almost all of them wear something branded, and almost all of it works hard in all weathers.

What they wear is a uniform before it is PPE. A delivery driver is the most visible, most frequent face a courier company has, meeting Corby customers at the door dozens of times a day, so a clean, branded polo, softshell or fleece is what makes a driver and a firm look professional. Around it sit the practical layers: waterproofs for the weather, hi-vis vests for the roadside and the depot, and safety boots for loading and yard work.

And the work runs year-round and all-weather, which makes the kit a layering system: an embroidered polo in summer, a fleece and softshell through spring and autumn, a waterproof over the top in winter, and a hi-vis vest whenever a driver is at the kerbside or in a yard. Every layer carries the same branding, so a Corby driver looks consistent whatever the round and the weather throw at them. And the buyers divide two ways: a fleet or depot operator kitting dozens or hundreds wants a trade account, volume pricing and consistency, and an owner-driver or small courier firm wants to pick a few branded items and order them direct, online, the same day.

Branded polos, softshells and fleeces are the essentials, with waterproofs, hi-vis vests, safety boots and caps alongside across the town's doorsteps, estates and rail-served logistics parks.
