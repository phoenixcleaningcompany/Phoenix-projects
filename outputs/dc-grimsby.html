Kitting the people who deliver Grimsby and North East Lincolnshire, door to door

A major port and food-processing town, Grimsby runs its delivery trade along the A180 and the A16, with the Europarc business park sitting between the ports of Grimsby and Immingham and feeding straight onto the A180 and M180 into the national network. The Port of Grimsby, the Great Coates and Pyewipe industrial estate and the South Humberside Industrial Estate make up the bulk of the town's warehouse and distribution space, putting a large share of the country's consumers within a few hours' drive of the Humber bank.

Grimsby is the heart of the UK seafood cluster, with the bulk of the nation's fish processed here, so a great deal of the courier and same-day work moves chilled and time-sensitive food consignments off Europarc and the fish docks alongside national carrier parcel rounds out of the estates. Independent multidrop firms and owner-drivers cover the terraced streets, the seafront at Cleethorpes and the runs out to Scunthorpe and across to Hull. Almost all of them wear something branded, and almost all of it works hard in all weathers.

The kit is a uniform first and PPE second. A driver is the most visible, most frequent face a Grimsby courier firm has, at the door dozens of times a day, so a clean, branded polo, softshell or fleece is what makes the driver and the firm look professional, with the practical layers around it: waterproofs for the weather, hi-vis vests for roadside and depot, and safety boots for loading and yard work.

The round is year-round and all-weather, so the kit works as a layering system: an embroidered polo in summer, a fleece and softshell through spring and autumn, a waterproof over the top in winter, and a hi-vis vest whenever a driver is at the kerbside or in a yard, every layer carrying the same branding so a Grimsby driver looks consistent in any weather. And the buyers divide two ways: a fleet or depot operator kitting dozens or hundreds wants a trade account, volume pricing and consistency, and an owner-driver or small courier firm wants to pick a few branded items and order them direct, online, the same day.

Branded polos, softshells and fleeces do the everyday work, backed by waterproofs, hi-vis vests, safety boots and caps across the town's docks, estates and ring-road hubs.
