What does that wash symbol mean?

Every garment ships with its washing instructions written in five little pictograms — and workwear punishes you for guessing, because the wrong wash strips flame resistance, fades hi-vis below its class and delaminates waterproofs while everything still looks fine. Questions

Care label FAQ

What does the number in the wash tub mean?

The maximum wash temperature in degrees Celsius — it's a ceiling, not a target. A bar under the tub means use a gentler, reduced-agitation cycle at that temperature; two bars, gentler still.

What does the triangle with two lines mean?

Oxygen or non-chlorine bleach only. Oxygen-based whiteners are fine; chlorine bleach is prohibited — on poly-cotton it causes yellowing and fibre damage rather than whitening.

Why do hi-vis labels say do not tumble dry?

Tumble heat accelerates fading of the fluorescent dye and degrades the retroreflective tape — both of which the garment's EN ISO 20471 class depends on. Line drying preserves the certification; the dryer quietly erodes it.

What happens if you ignore a care label on PPE?

The garment can drop below the performance its standard promises while looking perfectly fine — faded hi-vis that no longer meets its class, FR treatment stripped by the wrong detergent, waterproof membranes cooked by tumble heat. With protective kit, the care label is part of the safety spec.
