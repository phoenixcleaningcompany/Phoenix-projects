What Corby works in

From steel town to the nation's warehouse

Corby made its name in steel and tube, and metals engineering is still here — but the town's modern economy is logistics and distribution. Sat on the A14/M1 corridor at the heart of the "Golden Triangle", it's one of the country's densest clusters of distribution centres, including a lot of chilled and frozen cold-chain capacity, alongside serious food & drink manufacturing.

That mix sets the workwear. Corby kits out for movement, visibility and cold — pick/pack floors, yard traffic and freezer aisles — a completely different problem from a flame or chemical hazard. Hi-vis, footwear grip and thermal rating are what matter here.

Corby workwear FAQ

What workwear do warehouse workers in Corby need?

Corby's distribution centres run on high-visibility clothing to EN ISO 20471 (usually Class 2 or 3 where forklifts and yard traffic mix), S3 safety footwear to EN ISO 20345 for wet and pierce-risk floors, and cut-resistant gloves to EN 388 for handling. Chilled and frozen DCs add EN 342 cold-store thermals and EN 511 cold-rated gloves. Bump caps are common where racking is low.

Do you deliver workwear to Corby and Northamptonshire?

Yes. iNeedWorkwear is a nationwide supplier with next-working-day delivery to NN postcodes — Corby, the Midlands Logistics Park, Earlstrees, Willowbrook and the wider Northamptonshire estates. We're not a shop in the town; we supply and ship, with trade accounts, managed reorders and logo embroidery.

What thermals do cold-store staff in Corby need?

Chilled and frozen distribution centres require insulated clothing rated to EN 342 for cold environments, worn as base and mid layers under a hi-vis over-layer, plus EN 511 cold-rated gloves. Sizing tends to run larger to layer over normal kit, which is why we hold standing sizing packs for sites with high agency turnover.
