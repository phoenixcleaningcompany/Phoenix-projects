Privacy

No cookies. No tracking.

Most privacy policies are long because the site is doing a lot with your data. This one is short because we aren't doing anything with it.

The short version

What this site does with your data

0
Cookies set by this site — none, of any kind
0
Analytics, pixels or tracking scripts running
0
Forms — we collect no personal data through this site

ineedworkwear.uk is a plain reading site. Every page is static: no cookie banners because there are no cookies to warn you about, no consent boxes because there's nothing to consent to. Fonts and images are served from this domain, so reading our guides sends no request to any third party.

The full version

The detail, such as it is

If you contact us

The contact page lists a phone number and an email address. If you ring or email, we'll obviously see what you send — your message, your email address, anything you attach. We use it to answer you, keep it as long as an ordinary business conversation needs, and don't add you to marketing lists or share it with anyone.

Server logs

Like almost every website, the server this site runs on keeps standard technical logs (IP address, page requested, time) for security and error-fixing. They're routine, short-lived and not used to profile anyone.

The shop is separate

Buying happens at ineedworkwear.com, which runs on Shopify and has its own privacy policy and cookie controls — orders need cookies for things like a basket. That policy is linked from the shop itself.

Your rights

UK GDPR gives you rights over personal data — access, correction, deletion and the rest. Given the above, the only personal data we're likely to hold is an email you sent us; if you'd like it deleted, ask and it's done. Email hello@ineedworkwear.com or call 01597 730142. If you're unhappy with how we handle something, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

If this ever changes

If we ever add analytics or anything else that touches visitor data, this page changes first and a consent banner appears where the law requires one. Until then: browse freely.

Last reviewed July 2026. Questions about this policy: hello@ineedworkwear.com.
