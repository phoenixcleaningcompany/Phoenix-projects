Area guide · Hull

Workwear in Hull: composites, caravans and quaysides.

Hull's two flagship products — offshore wind turbine blades and holiday caravans — share a material: glass-reinforced composite. That makes fine dust the city's defining occupational hazard, and disposable coveralls with properly rated respirators the kit that matters most, before the docks and the food plants add their own lists.

The local economy

What Hull actually makes

The blade factory on Alexandra Dock builds some of the largest composite structures manufactured anywhere in the UK, and the caravan and park-home industry across Hull and the East Riding builds thousands of GRP-bodied units a year — between them employing a workforce that laminates, trims, sands and finishes composite all day. Add the port (ferries, containers, project cargo for offshore wind), a substantial food-processing base, and the hospitals and care economy of a city of a quarter-million, and Hull's kit list writes itself in four columns.

The detail that matters

Composite dust writes the spec

Sanding cured GRP produces fine, itchy, respirable dust — unpleasant on skin, genuinely harmful in lungs. The working answer is a type 5/6 disposable coverall taped at the cuffs, FFP3 respirators where extraction can't capture everything, and nitrile gloves under the work gloves for resin days. Two buying rules follow: disposables are consumed by the case, not the garment (see disposable vs reusable), and respirator spend is wasted without face-fit — a leaking FFP3 protects like an FFP0.

Everything else in the city is more conventional — but the estuary adds its tax: dockside hi-vis lives in wind-driven rain, so fluorescence and tape condition retire garments here faster than wear does.

Questions

Hull kit FAQ

What PPE is needed for fibreglass and composite work?

Type 5/6 disposable coveralls (dust-tight, splash-resistant), an FFP3 respirator for sanding and trimming where extraction doesn't capture everything, nitrile gloves against resins, and eye protection. Coveralls are single-session consumables — re-wearing a dusty one defeats the point.

What's the difference between FFP2 and FFP3 for composite dust?

Filtration efficiency — FFP3 filters the highest proportion of fine particulate and is the usual specification for sanding composite. Either way the mask only performs if it seals: face-fit testing is what turns the rating on the box into protection on the face.
