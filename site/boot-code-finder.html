Questions

Finder FAQ

Why does the tool never suggest SB?

SB is the bare toe-cap minimum with none of the antistatic, heel-cushioning or closed-seat features that S1 adds — and the price difference is trivial. In practice there's no working environment where SB is the right specification over S1.

What if my site just says 'S3 boots required'?

Then S3 it is — site rules override risk-based logic, and many principal contractors mandate S3 with ankle support as a blanket policy. The tool is most useful where nobody has specified anything and someone has to decide.

Are safety trainers a code of their own?

No — a safety trainer is a style, not a rating. An S1P trainer carries exactly the same certified toe cap and midsole as an S1P boot. What you give up is ankle coverage and weather resistance, which is why trainers suit the dry-indoor answers.
