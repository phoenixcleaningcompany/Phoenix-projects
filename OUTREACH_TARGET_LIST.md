# Facilities / Block Management — Target List (starter)

## Why size matters here
National giants (HC-One, Care UK, Bupa, Kier, Mainstay) are excluded deliberately. They run centralized procurement/framework contracts built for large accredited suppliers, and their volume would exceed your realistic capacity (~22-26 jobs/month) even if you won the work. The right target is a company managing somewhere between roughly 5 and 30 sites — big enough that winning the relationship is worth the outreach effort, small enough to still have a reachable decision-maker and to fit inside your capacity.

## Tier 1 — Regional/independent, worth direct outreach

**Facilities management companies serving education/care sectors:**
- Elevate FM — facilities management for schools ([elevatefm.co.uk](https://www.elevatefm.co.uk/sectors/education-services))
- TGC Facilities — NHS Trusts, care/nursing homes, GP surgeries, schools/colleges/academies ([tgc-uk.com](https://www.tgc-uk.com/))
- Guardian — property managers, student accommodation, care homes ([guardian.uk.com](https://www.guardian.uk.com/))
- Heb FM — NHS clinics, academies, care providers, retail property groups ([heb-group.com](https://www.heb-group.com/facilities-management))

**Block/student accommodation management (mid-size, not the 80,000-home giants):**
- StudentFM — block management for student accommodation, compliance oversight ([studentfm.co.uk](https://studentfm.co.uk/leaseholders/))
- Seraph — PBSA, build-to-rent, portfolio property management ([seraph.pm](https://www.seraph.pm/))
- Cloud Student Homes — block management, landlord services ([cloudstudenthomes.co.uk](https://cloudstudenthomes.co.uk/))
- Rendall & Rittner — residential/mixed-use block and estate management (worth checking their portfolio size regionally before assuming fit)

## Tier 2 — Independent/regional care home groups
This needs more targeted research than a general web search surfaces (search results skew toward the top 10 giants). Next step: search sites like carehomeprofessional.com and CareRadar's operator listings, filtered to groups with roughly 5-30 homes in regions near your existing client clusters, rather than the top-10-by-revenue lists.

## What I haven't done, and why

LinkedIn is usually the fastest route to a real name and title once a target company is picked.
