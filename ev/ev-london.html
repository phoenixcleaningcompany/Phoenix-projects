Kitting the events capital

London is the UK's events capital. The West End theatres, arenas like the O2 and Wembley, the Royal Albert Hall and Alexandra Palace, and concert venues from Brixton Academy to the Roundhouse run shows all year, while the summer fills the parks with festivals from BST Hyde Park and All Points East to Wireless and the Notting Hill Carnival. Behind every one of them sits a trade of event production companies, staging and rigging crews and front-of-house teams.

And that trade runs at every size, which shapes what a workwear supplier has to do. Major venues, festival operators and production companies run big crews and large seasonal teams and buy through procurement, while independent event crews, promoters, mobile entertainers and one-van operators need just a few branded pieces. A supplier here has to serve the big festival and the single crew alike, which is why we run trade accounts and direct online ordering side by side.

And the kit splits two ways, which is what makes event workwear its own thing. Front-of-house staff, the stewards, bar, box office and hospitality teams, need branded polos so the audience can see at a glance who is official and who to ask. The crew, riggers and production teams need hi-vis, fleeces, waterproofs, cargo trousers and safety boots for the physical, outdoor, often overnight work of the get-in, the show and the breakdown.

And because events and festivals are very seasonal, it is a repeat-purchase trade with a big intake each year, and the range is built to reorder easily. Polos, hi-vis, fleeces, waterproofs and safety boots, branded in-house with the operator name, are the whole kit, and we hold your logo on file so the next season's crew and the next new starter match what the team already wears.
