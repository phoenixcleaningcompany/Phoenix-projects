# Facilities / Block Management Outreach

## Target profile
- Facilities management companies servicing multiple care homes, nurseries, schools, or sports clubs
- Block/property management companies overseeing residential blocks or student accommodation with communal laundries
- Secondary (referral partners, not cold outreach): fire risk assessors, catering equipment engineers

## Why this pitch leads with LEV, not TR19
LEV testing is enforced by the HSE under COSHH — a genuine legal requirement, not "best practice." TR19 extraction cleaning is tied to fire risk assessments and insurance validity but is "not always enforced." Leading with the harder legal fact is more credible and less salesy than a generic "book a clean" pitch.

- TR19 extraction cleaning is tied to fire risk assessments and insurance validity, and gets missed when no single person is tracking it across multiple sites.

## Phone script — cold call opener

"Hi, is that [Name]? I run a compliance cleaning business — TR19 kitchen extraction and LEV testing — and I work with a couple of facilities/block management companies on a rolling schedule across their sites. Wondering if that's worth 5 minutes for the sites you manage — are you the right person for that, or is there someone else who handles compliance/maintenance scheduling?"

If they engage:
- LEV testing = HSE-enforced legal requirement (COSHH), often overdue without anyone noticing across a portfolio
- Already run recurring schedules for multi-site clients (mention scale without overstating)
- Offer a no-obligation quote across their portfolio, single point of contact for all sites

## LinkedIn message — shorter version

Hi [Name] — I handle TR19 kitchen extraction cleaning and LEV/COSHH testing for care homes, schools and managed sites nationwide, currently on a recurring schedule for multiple sites via a facilities/block management partner. Worth a quick chat about coverage for the sites you manage? No pressure either way.

## Notes
- Don't name the existing facilities/block management clients without their permission — refer to scale generically ("multiple schools," "several sites") unless you get an explicit OK to use them as a named reference.
- Track every contact attempt and outcome in one place (spreadsheet is enough) — no-response is the default outcome, so the win is in follow-up cadence (e.g. a second touch 2 weeks later), not a single email.
