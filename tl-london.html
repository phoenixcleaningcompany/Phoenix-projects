Kitting the visitor economy of the capital

London is one of the world's great tourism destinations. The London Eye, the Tower of London, the West End, world-class museums and galleries, the river and the royal parks draw tens of millions of visitors a year, while the big theme parks just outside the city, from Thorpe Park to Chessington and Legoland, pull in millions more. Around them sits a vast trade of hotels, tour operators, activity centres, attractions and guides, all run by customer-facing staff.

And the trade runs at every size, which shapes what a workwear supplier has to do. Major attractions, theme parks and hotel groups run big teams, take on large seasonal intakes and buy through procurement, while independent activity centres, tour operators, museums and guides need just a few branded pieces. A supplier here has to serve the big park and the one-person guide alike, which is why we run trade accounts and direct online ordering side by side.

And the kit does two jobs at once. It makes staff instantly identifiable to visitors, so a guest at a busy attraction or park can spot who works there and who to ask, which turns even a seasonal hire into a recognisable part of the team. And it is layered for all-weather outdoor work across a long season: polos for the warm days, fleeces and softshells as it cools, waterproofs for the wet, and hi-vis for car parks, events and outdoor activities.

Because tourism and leisure is seasonal, it is also a repeat-purchase trade with a big intake each year, and the range is built to reorder easily. Polos, fleeces, softshells, waterproofs and hi-vis, branded in-house with the operator name, are the whole kit, and we hold your logo on file so the next season's staff and the next new starter match what the team already wears.
