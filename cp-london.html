Kitting the corporate capital

London is the UK's corporate and professional-services capital. The City and Canary Wharf hold the banks, insurers and financial-services firms, the Inns of Court and the magic-circle law firms run the legal sector, the big accountancies and global consultancies base their head offices here, and Shoreditch and King's Cross anchor a vast technology and fintech scene. It is a city of professionals in suits, but ones who regularly step into branded kit.

And because most of these firms wear no uniform day to day, corporate workwear here is a brand exercise rather than a daily one. It is bought by procurement, office and brand managers for specific occasions, and when it does appear it has to match the firm's brand exactly, because a branded polo or softshell represents the firm in public just as much as the letterhead or the website does.

So the kit is for the moments staff step out of the suit: away days and team days, conferences and exhibitions, sponsored and fundraising events, site and client visits, and welcome packs for new starters. It has to be on-brand, with the right logo, the right colours and embroidered to guidelines, and it has to be account-managed, so a brand pack held on file reproduces exactly the same look every time across every office and team.

That is why the range is deliberately narrow and the value is consistency and service. Branded polos, shirts, softshells and fleeces, embroidered in-house to your guidelines, are the whole kit, and a held brand pack, managed reorders and a single point of contact mean each office, team and event matches the brand the firm has spent years building.
